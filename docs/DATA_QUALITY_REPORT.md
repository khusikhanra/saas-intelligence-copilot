## Join cardinality (why naive joins double count)

- subscriptions per account avg: **10.0**
- tickets per account avg: **4.0**
- usage rows per subscription avg: **5.0**
- churn events per account avg: **1.2**

Aggregations across child tables therefore pre-aggregate to account grain (`account_features`) before joining.

## Issues

| DQ-04 | High | feature_usage | 19,432 (77.7%) | **Feature usage dated outside its subscription window**. 19142 usage rows predate the subscription start and 290 postdate its end. | Usage is not lifecycle-aligned: onboarding/activation, pre-churn usage decline and time-based engagement cannot be measured validly. | Usage is used only as lifetime per-account aggregates (total events, distinct features); no time-aligned engagement claims. |
| DQ-03 | High | subscriptions | 496 (99.2%) | **Many concurrent open subscriptions per account (overlapping periods)**. 496 accounts hold more than one open paid subscription at the same time (avg 7.6 per account; accounts average 10 subscription rows). | Summing active subscription rows gives very high MRR (average per account is far above the plan price card).
